GOVERNMENT OF INDIA & NATIONAL HIGHWAYS AUTHORITY OF INDIA
MODEL CONCESSION AGREEMENT — FOUR-LANING OF NATIONAL HIGHWAY (BOT-TOLL)
Execution Jurisdiction: New Delhi, India

1.1 Grant of Concession. Subject to and in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement, the Authority hereby grants to the Concessionaire the exclusive right, licence, and authority for a period of 25 (twenty-five) years to finance, design, construct, operate, and maintain the Four-Laning of National Highway Section (the "Project Highway").

7.1 Escrow Account and Revenue Custody. The Concessionaire shall establish an Escrow Account with an Escrow Bank in accordance with Schedule G. All inflows including toll revenues, grant funds, and equity contributions shall be deposited exclusively into the Escrow Account.

15.1 Commercial Operation Date. The Concessionaire shall achieve Commercial Operation Date (COD) within 900 days from the Appointed Date in accordance with the construction milestones.

15.4 Punitive Daily Delay Penalty. If COD is not achieved on or before the scheduled COD, the Concessionaire shall pay to the Authority liquidated damages at the rate of 1.0% (one percent) of the Total Project Cost for each day of delay, immediately recoverable from the Performance Security without proof of actual loss.

23.1 Concessionaire Default Termination. In the event of Concessionaire Default, the Authority shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement and immediately take over the Project Highway.

23.2 Asymmetric Compensation and Statutory Remedy Waiver. Upon termination for Concessionaire Default, the Authority shall pay only 50% of the Senior Debt Due. The Concessionaire hereby irrevocably waives all statutory rights, remedies, and claims under Indian law, and agrees that it shall not institute any suit, arbitration, or legal proceeding against the Authority before any court or tribunal.

28.1 Dispute Resolution by Arbitration. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration seated in New Delhi under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

28.3 Exclusion of Pre-Litigation Mediation. The Parties expressly covenant that Section 5 of the Mediation Act, 2023 shall not apply to any dispute hereunder. The Parties are strictly prohibited from initiating or participating in pre-litigation mediation, and any attempt to invoke statutory mediation shall constitute an immediate material default.

32.1 Unilateral Variation by Authority. The Authority shall have the unfettered right to unilaterally alter the Concession Period, adjust the toll collection schedule, and reallocate revenue share percentages at any time during the Concession Period without the consent of or compensation to the Concessionaire.
