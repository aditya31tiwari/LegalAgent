INDIAN EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

1.1 Appointment. The Company appoints the Executive as Chief Technology Officer in India.

6.1 Confidentiality. The Executive shall protect confidential information and trade secrets during and after employment.

8.1 Post-Termination Non-Compete. For 24 months following termination of employment, the Executive shall not compete with the Company or advise any competing business in India.

11.1 Survival. Section 6.1 and Section 8.1 shall survive termination for 24 months.

14.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
