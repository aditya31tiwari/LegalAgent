# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT & RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AGREEMENT

**Executed at Mumbai, Maharashtra &middot; Confidential Legal Instrument**

---

## PARTIES

1. **Apex Financial Technologies India Private Limited**, having its corporate office at Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051, Maharashtra (the **"Company"**); and
2. **Executive (Chief Technology Officer)**, residing at Mumbai, Maharashtra.

---

## SECTION 1: APPOINTMENT & EXECUTIVE DUTIES

### 1.1 Engagement
Company engages Executive as Chief Technology Officer to lead architectural engineering and quantitative algorithms. Executive owes fiduciary duties of loyalty, good faith, and confidential stewardship.

---

## SECTION 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

### 4.1 Inventions & Work for Hire
All algorithmic models, software architectures, patentable workflows, and code repositories conceived by Executive during employment shall constitute "work made for hire" under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, and are irrevocably assigned to Company worldwide.

---

## SECTION 6: CONFIDENTIALITY & PROPRIETARY SECRETS

### 6.1 Non-Disclosure
Executive shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding proprietary trading strategies and algorithmic weights during employment and indefinitely thereafter.

---

## SECTION 8: RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

### 8.1 Post-Termination Non-Compete
For twenty-four (24) months following termination of employment for any reason, Executive shall not directly or indirectly engage in, advise, consult for, or manage any competing fintech or quantitative trading venture in India.
*(Statutory Violation: Void ab initio under Section 27, Indian Contract Act 1872 & Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan, 2006).*

---

## SECTION 11: SURVIVAL

### 11.1 Survival of Restraints
Section 6 (Confidentiality) and Section 8 (Non-Compete) shall survive termination of employment for the full duration specified therein.
*(Dependency Risk: Cross-clause dependency perpetuating void non-compete restraint).*

---

## SECTION 14: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION

### 14.1 Governing Law
Governed by the substantive laws of India. Courts in Mumbai, Maharashtra have exclusive jurisdiction.
