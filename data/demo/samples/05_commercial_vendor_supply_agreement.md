## SECTION 5: DELAY DAMAGES & PENALTIES

### 5.1 Punitive Liquidated Delay Damages
Any delay in delivery beyond 3 (three) business days incurs automatic liquidated damages of 30% (thirty percent) of the total order value, immediately forfeited from Purchaser's security deposit.
*(Statutory Violation: Unenforceable penalty in terrorem under Section 74, Indian Contract Act 1872 & Kailash Nath Associates v. DDA, 2015).*

## SECTION 8: LIMITATION OF SUPPLIER LIABILITY

### 8.2 Overall Liability Cap
Supplier's total cumulative aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall be limited to 10% (ten percent) of total fees paid under the applicable Purchase Order.
*(Contradiction: Direct clash between Section 5.1 30% penalty and Section 8.2 10% liability ceiling).*
