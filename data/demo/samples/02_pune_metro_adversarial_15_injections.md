# CONCESSION AGREEMENT FOR PUNE METRO LINE III (HINJAWADI — SHIVAJINAGAR)

**Government of Maharashtra &middot; Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (PMRDA)**
*Concession Document Ref: PMRDA/3316/CR 54/UD-7 &middot; Volume I–IV &middot; Executed September 21, 2019*

---

## PARTIES

1. **Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority**, constituted pursuant to Section 3 of the Maharashtra Metropolitan Region Development Authority Act, 2016, having its principal office at Maharaja Sayajirao Gaikwad Udyog Bhavan, Aundh, Pune 411007, Maharashtra (hereinafter called the **"Authority"**); and
2. **Pune IT City Metro Rail Limited**, a public limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 by the Consortium of TRIL Urban Transport Private Limited and Siemens Project Ventures GmbH, having its registered office at TACO House, Plot No. 20/B, Damle Path, Erandwane, Pune 411004, Maharashtra (hereinafter called the **"Concessionaire"**).

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**
(A) The Authority resolved to provide a mass rapid rail transit system of 23.205 km on elevated dual-track viaduct from Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar on Design, Build, Finance, Operate and Transfer (DBFOT) basis in accordance with the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978 and the Metro Rail Policy, 2017.
(B) Following competitive bidding pursuant to Request for Proposals (RFP), the Consortium of TRIL Urban Transport Private Limited and Siemens Project Ventures GmbH was selected and issued Letter of Award dated October 3, 2018.
(C) The Selected Bidder incorporated the Concessionaire as a Special Purpose Vehicle to execute the Concession Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Definitions
In this Agreement, capitalized terms shall have the meanings assigned:
* **"Applicable Laws"** means all statutes, enactments, rules, regulations, ordinances, and court judgments of India and the State of Maharashtra in force from time to time.
* **"Commercial Operation Date" (COD)** means the date on which the Completion Certificate is issued by the Independent Engineer pursuant to Article 15.
* **"Concession Period"** means 35 (thirty-five) years commencing from the Appointed Date.
* **"Force Majeure Event"** shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Article 34.
* **"Performance Security"** means the unconditional bank guarantee provided to the Authority pursuant to Article 9.
* **"Right of Way"** means constructive possession of the Site free and clear of all Encumbrances.

### 1.2 Principles of Interpretation
References to any statute include statutory amendments or re-enactments. Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.

---

## ARTICLE 3: GRANT OF CONCESSION

### 3.1 The Concession
Subject to and in accordance with this Agreement, the Authority grants to the Concessionaire the exclusive right and concession for 35 (thirty-five) years to finance, design, construct, operate, maintain, and collect user fares along Pune Metro Line III (Hinjawadi–Shivajinagar).

### 3.2 Non-Exclusivity and Other Transit Routes
The Concessionaire acknowledges that the Authority may sponsor other transit networks in Pune. The Concessionaire retains the liberty to bid for and execute other infrastructure projects in India.

---

## ARTICLE 15: COMMERCIAL OPERATION & DELAY LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

### 15.5 Damages for Delay
If the Concessionaire fails to achieve COD on or before the Scheduled Completion Date, it shall pay liquidated damages at 0.1% (zero point one percent) of Performance Security per day of delay, subject to an aggregate ceiling of 20% (twenty percent) of Performance Security, representing a genuine pre-estimate of loss under Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

---

## ARTICLE 18: PASSENGER CHARTER & DATA GOVERNANCE

### 18.1 Automated Fare Collection (AFC)
All ticketing telemetry and passenger travel logs collected through the AFC system shall be processed strictly for transit operations and interchange settlement under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). Commercial monetization of passenger personal data without affirmative statutory consent is strictly prohibited.

---

## ARTICLE 41: LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

### 41.1 Reciprocal Indemnities
The Concessionaire and Authority shall mutually indemnify each other against claims arising from breach, bodily injury, property damage, or gross negligence resulting from their respective operations.

### 41.5 No Consequential Damages
Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, special, punitive, or consequential damages, or loss of anticipated revenue.

---

## ARTICLE 44: DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ARBITRATION

### 44.1 Amicable Conciliation & Mediation
Disputes shall first be submitted to good-faith conciliation and statutory pre-litigation mediation under The Mediation Act, 2023.

### 44.3 Arbitration
Unresolved disputes shall be finally settled by a three-member arbitral tribunal under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 with seat at Pune, Maharashtra, India.

---

## ARTICLE 47: GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION

### 47.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed exclusively by the laws of India. The courts of Pune and the High Court of Judicature at Bombay shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
