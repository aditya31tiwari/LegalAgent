# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (IT & CLOUD SERVICES)

**Agreement Reference: CL-BRL-MSA-2024-001**
*Executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka &middot; Effective April 1, 2024*

---

## PARTIES

1. **CloudLogic Enterprise Solutions India Private Limited**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with registered office at Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru 560103, Karnataka (the **"Vendor"**); and
2. **Bharat Retail Logistics Limited**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with corporate office at MG Road, Bengaluru 560001, Karnataka (the **"Customer"**).

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**
(A) Vendor provides enterprise cloud infrastructure, managed database hosting, and high-availability SaaS platforms under ISO/IEC 27001 standards.
(B) Customer operates omni-channel logistics networks and desires to engage Vendor to deliver Tier-3 cloud services.

**NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:**

---

## SECTION 1: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Definitions
Capitalized terms used herein:
* **"Customer Data"** means all electronic data, customer personal data, transaction telemetry, and confidential files uploaded or processed through Vendor's cloud platform.
* **"Data Fiduciary"** and **"Data Processor"** have meanings assigned under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).
* **"Service Levels"** means the availability benchmarks set forth in Section 3.2.

---

## SECTION 3: SCOPE OF SERVICES & SLAS

### 3.2 Service Level Commitments
Vendor warrants that the Cloud Services shall maintain an aggregate monthly uptime availability of not less than 99.9% (ninety-nine point nine percent), measured 24/7/365, excluding Scheduled Maintenance Windows on Sundays between 02:00 IST and 04:00 IST.

---

## SECTION 4: SERVICE CREDITS & REBATES

### 4.1 Service Credit Schedule
Subject to Section 3.2, in the event of availability shortfalls, Customer may deduct service credits up to a maximum aggregate ceiling of 5% (five percent) of total monthly billing for any single billing cycle.

---

## SECTION 7: DATA PROTECTION & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

### 7.1 Statutory Recognition
Customer is Data Fiduciary and Vendor is Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Vendor implements AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

### 7.4 Data Breach Regulatory Liability Cap
Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to any personal data breach or regulatory proceedings under DPDPA 2023 shall in no event exceed professional fees paid by Customer in the prior 3 (three) months.
*(Statutory Risk: DPDPA regulatory penalties up to INR 250 Crore cannot be contractually capped).*

---

## SECTION 8: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

### 8.1 Liability Ceiling
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Indian law, neither Party's total aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall exceed INR 15,00,000 (Rupees Fifteen Lakhs only). Consequential and indirect damages are expressly excluded.

---

## SECTION 12: INDEMNIFICATION

### 12.1 Uncapped Intellectual Property Indemnity
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against all third-party claims, liabilities, and damages alleging that Cloud Services infringe any patent, copyright, or trademark, without any financial limitation whatsoever and notwithstanding Section 8.1.
*(Contradiction: Direct clash between Section 8.1 INR 15,00,000 liability ceiling and Section 12.1 uncapped indemnity).*

### 12.3 Regulatory Fines Indemnity
Customer shall indemnify Vendor from all fines imposed by the Data Protection Board of India resulting from Customer's improper consent records.

---

## SECTION 15: TERM AND TERMINATION

### 15.1 Term & Convenience Termination
Initial term of 3 (three) years. Either Party may terminate for convenience upon 60 (sixty) days prior written notice.

### 15.4 Indefinite Survival
Provisions of Section 7 (Data Protection), Section 8 (Liability Cap), and Section 12 (Indemnity) shall survive termination indefinitely.

---

## SECTION 18: GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 18.1 Governing Law & Jurisdiction
Governed by the laws of India. Courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 18.2 Dispute Resolution & Mediation Bypass
Disputes resolved by sole arbitrator in Bengaluru under Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The Parties explicitly agree to exclude and bypass pre-litigation commercial mediation under The Mediation Act, 2023.
*(Statutory Risk: Mandatory mediation under The Mediation Act 2023 cannot be contractually bypassed).*
