# MODEL CONCESSION AGREEMENT — FOUR-LANING OF NATIONAL HIGHWAYS (BOT-TOLL)

**National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) &middot; Ministry of Road Transport & Highways**

---

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS & CONCESSION GRANT

### 1.1 Concession Grant
NHAI grants Concessionaire the exclusive right and authority for 25 (twenty-five) years to finance, construct, operate, and maintain the Four-Laning of National Highway on Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT-Toll) mode.

---

## ARTICLE 15: COMMERCIAL OPERATION & DELAY DAMAGES

### 15.3 Liquidated Damages for Delay
Concessionaire pays liquidated damages at 0.1% per week of Total Project Cost (capped at 10% of TPC) for delay in achieving Commercial Operation Date.

---

## ARTICLE 23: TERMINATION COMPENSATION ASYMMETRY

### 23.1 Concessionaire Default Termination
On Concessionaire Default, NHAI pays only 90% (ninety percent) of Senior Debt Due; all Sponsor equity and subordinated shareholder debt is entirely forfeited.

### 23.3 NHAI Default Termination
On NHAI Default, NHAI pays: (i) 100% of Debt Due; (ii) Adjusted Equity; and (iii) 150% of Sponsor's Base Return across the remaining concession period.
*(Commercial Asymmetry: Extreme financial distress risk litigated under Indian Contract Act Section 74).*

---

## ARTICLE 28: TIERED DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 28.1 Dispute Resolution Board & Arbitration
Disputes referred first to Dispute Resolution Board (DRB) under Schedule G. DRB recommendations bind parties unless challenged by arbitration within 30 days in New Delhi under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
