Standard Single-Clause LLM (Failure Mode)

Why Isolated Review Fails

Standard legaltech LLMs examine contract sections in isolation. When reviewing a liability cap clause, the LLM summarizes it as a valid limitation of liability. When reviewing an uncapped indemnification covenant 40 pages later, it summarizes it as a standard indemnity. It fails to identify that the two provisions destroy each other's legal certainty.

LegalAgent Cross-Clause GraphRAG (Solution)

How Graph Topology Catches Contradictions

LegalAgent constructs a relational interdependency graph G=(V,E). Candidate selection links interdependent label pairs (Liability × Indemnity, Term × Survival). The deterministic cross-clause reasoning engine verifies the conflict, cites governing Indian case law, and surfaces the finding with zero hallucination.
