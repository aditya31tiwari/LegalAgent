Capabilities

What LegalAgent Does

An AI-powered contract review engine that goes beyond single-clause analysis to detect cross-clause contradictions and map the full risk topology of a contract.

Cross-Clause Graph Analysis

Builds an attributed knowledge graph G=(V,E) of clause interdependencies, surfacing conflicts invisible to linear document review.

Indian Statutory Compliance

Flags clauses void under Indian Contract Act (Section 27 non-competes, Section 73/74 penalties), DPDPA 2023, and BNS 2023.

Contradiction Detection

Identifies when a liability cap in one clause is nullified by uncapped indemnity in another, and similar inter-clause conflicts.

Legal NLP Models

Uses domain-specific embeddings (InLegal-SBERT, InLegalBERT) trained on Indian legal text for superior semantic clause matching.

Visual Risk Topology

Interactive clause interdependency graph with colour-coded edges for conflicts, statutory violations, cross-references, and semantic links.

Audit Memorandum Export

Generates executive-ready legal risk memos with statutory citations, affected provisions, and recommended amendments.

Process

How It Works

A six-stage pipeline from raw contract text to actionable risk intelligence.

01

Ingest & Parse

Upload contract text. The system segments it into individual clauses and identifies section boundaries.

02

Classify & Label

Each clause is classified against CUAD taxonomy — liability, indemnity, non-compete, termination, and 37 more categories.

03

Select Candidates

BM25 and dense retrieval (InLegal-SBERT) identify clause pairs likely to contain contradictions or dependencies.

04

Analyse & Report

Cross-clause analysis checks for conflicts, statutory violations, and generates findings with citations and remedies.

Jurisdiction

Built for Indian Commercial Law

LegalAgent encodes key provisions of Indian statutes that affect commercial contract validity and enforceability.

Indian Contract Act, 1872

Section 27 (void non-competes), Section 73/74 (penalty vs. damages), Sections 124-125 (indemnity obligations). Post-termination restraints are void ab initio regardless of reasonableness.

Core Statute

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Contractual consent clauses must comply with DPDPA's affirmative consent framework. Blanket data processing permissions may be unenforceable.

New Legislation

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

Replaced the Indian Penal Code. Contract penalty clauses referencing IPC sections need re-mapping to BNS equivalents.

New Legislation

Mediation Act, 2023

Mandatory pre-litigation mediation affects dispute resolution clauses. Arbitration-first provisions may need a mediation precondition.
