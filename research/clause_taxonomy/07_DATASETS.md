## 2. Plain Answer on Public Indian Contract-Clause Datasets

**No public, human-annotated, clause-level Indian commercial contract classification dataset exists today.**
- `IL-TUR` and `InLegalNER` cover Indian *court judgments* (and are gated).
- `mhardik003/15k-indian-legal_contracts_and_agreements` is an empty stub.
- `azazelovesH100/indian-inhouse-clause-corpus` is ungated CC-BY-4.0, but is explicitly **LLM-generated synthetic templates**, not real contracts.

Therefore, for the 18-hour viva deadline, the **only defensible, zero-leakage strategy** is:
1. Retrain InLegalBERT on **CLAUSE-22** (re-mapped CUAD + LexGLUE LEDGAR, both gold, ungated, CC-BY-4.0, already on disk — 1 min rebuild + 45 min retrain).
2. Cover the 7 Indian/PPP-specific labels that have < 300 gold rows via **explicit, auditable deterministic rules** in `classification.py` (`known_gaps`), evaluated separately on Indian contracts rather than faked with synthetic training data.
3. Post-viva, harvest non-demo DEA / NITI Aayog MCAs + LEDGAR-Full long-tail headings (`Force Majeure`, `Liquidated Damages`, `Data Protection`) and silver-label with Gemini + human spot-check.
